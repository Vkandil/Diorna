# Code of Conduct

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be kind,
specific, and constructive; respect differing experience and identity; do not
publish another person's private information; and never use project spaces for
harassment, threats, or sexualised attention.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently exclude participants whose conduct is harmful. Report incidents
privately through the security/contact address configured by the repository
owner. Reports are handled promptly, confidentially, and without retaliation.
